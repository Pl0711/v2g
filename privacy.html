Privacy Policy for V2G - Gif maker

Last updated: November 24, 2026

V2G - Gif maker is developed by Pablo Orellana. The app turns videos, Live Photos and photo sequences into GIFs entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your videos, photos and GIFs

All GIF creation happens on your device, offline. The app never uploads your videos, photos or GIFs anywhere, and the developer never receives them. Videos and photos are chosen by you through the system photo picker, which does not give the app access to your full photo library. Saving a finished GIF to Photos uses add-only permission, requested the first time you save.

Information we collect

The app has no user accounts and no sign-up. It contains no analytics software, and the developer does not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you, and never sells data. Settings you choose in the app (such as your appearance preference) are stored on your device only and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

None. This version of the app contains no advertising and no advertising software. (A future update may add non-personalised banner ads; if it does, this policy will be updated and re-dated before that version is released.)

Network use

Other than Apple's own system services, the app makes no network calls. GIF creation works fully offline.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.

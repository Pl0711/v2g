V2G - Gif maker — Support

V2G turns videos, Live Photos and photo sequences into GIFs, entirely on your device — no account, no uploads, works offline.

Making a GIF

Tap Create, pick a video, Live Photo or a set of photos, trim and adjust it in the editor, then Export. Finished GIFs are kept in My GIFs inside the app when you tap Done; use Save to Photos on the result screen or from My GIFs to also keep a copy in your Photos library.

Why is my GIF file so large?

A GIF stores every frame as a full picture, so size grows with length, frame rate and dimensions. To make a smaller file: trim it shorter, choose the Low or Balanced quality tier, or lower the frame rate under More options in the editor. The size estimate in the editor is based on your actual preview render, so it should be close to the exported file.

Saving to Photos isn't working

The app asks for add-only Photos permission the first time you save. If you declined it, allow it under Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → V2G, or open the GIF in My GIFs and use Share instead.

Converting a GIF to video

Open any GIF from the export result screen or My GIFs and choose "Save as Video" to get an MP4 version.
